BEFORE, DURING, AND AFTER FLIGHT

Support that starts with each astronaut.

HALO AI learns what is usual for each person, checks in during the mission, and offers a little extra support when it may help.

01 / BEFORE FLIGHT

Before launch

Before launch, each astronaut takes short focus and memory tests and has a DTI scan, which shows brain connections. This gives HALO AI a personal starting point.

02 / DAILY CHECK-IN

Check in during flight

During the mission, quick check-ins help build a picture of each astronaut’s usual sleep, stress, and workload over time.

03 / EXTRA SUPPORT

Offer a timely activity

If a change continues, HALO AI gives an early heads-up and may suggest a short exercise. Sequential Recall, for example, helps someone practice remembering a series.

04 / AFTER FLIGHT

Continue the journey

After landing, mission check-ins and follow-up can help each astronaut reflect on their experience and transition home.

See how it goes

After the exercise, HALO AI checks whether results are moving back toward the astronaut’s usual level.

After the mission

Back on Earth, a second DTI scan is compared with the first scan and the check-ins from the mission.
