Far above Earth. Working for its future.

In this scenario, repeated crop failures have put Earth’s food supply under pressure. Four astronauts aboard the ISS are testing a closed-loop system that grows food while recycling water and nutrients. Their findings could help communities on Earth produce food with fewer resources.
